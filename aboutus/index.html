About Us

R-Fusion is a deep-tech materials company re-engineering high-performance fibers structurally for the next generation of advanced personal protective equipment.

Our core breakthrough lies in a proprietary process that extracts and fully disentangles ultra-high-functional fibers without relying on the harsh, degrading chemicals traditionally used by coating methods in advanced PPEs. The result is a scalable pathway to transform overlooked biological resources into advanced materials with exceptional performance potential.

Based in Boone, North Carolina, R-Fusion is proudly rooted in the Appalachian region. We are committed to bringing cutting-edge textile innovation and advanced manufacturing back to Western North Carolina, a region with a deep legacy in performance fabrics.

North Carolina also has one of the largest agricultural footprints in the United States, including extensive animal production and processing operations. These industries generate significant volumes of biological waste each year. R-Fusion sees this not as waste, but as an untapped materials resource that can be redirected into high-value applications through science, engineering, and scalable manufacturing.

Backed by the NCInnovation grant and Appalachian State University, R-Fusion is building from a strong foundation of regional talent, research excellence, and entrepreneurial momentum. Our mission is to create advanced materials that strengthen domestic supply chains, reduce waste, and establish Western North Carolina as a hub for the next era of textile technology.

We aim to build the future of protective textiles from the heart of Appalachia.
